Ponker privacy

Ponker is a free poker game with fake chips, made by Aidan Bailey for fun. Here's everything it keeps about you.

Playing as a guest

Nothing leaves your browser except the name you type and a random code that holds your seat if your connection drops. Your chips, settings and name are kept in your browser's local storage.

Signing in with Google or Discord

Ponker only asks Google or Discord who you are, as a number they give each account. It never sees or keeps your email address, your real name, your contacts or anything else. Alongside that number it keeps the display name you pick and the pixel picture you draw, both of which other players see at the table.

Signing in leaves a token in your browser so you stay signed in. The server only keeps a scrambled copy of it, and it runs out after 90 days.

Reports

If someone reports your picture, the report (who made it and when) is kept until it's been looked at. A picture that breaks the rules gets reset to the default.

Deleting it

Delete account in Profile removes your account, your name, your picture and your sessions straight away. Signing out on its own just forgets you on that device.

Where it lives

The game runs on Cloudflare and the site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads, and there's no tracking.
